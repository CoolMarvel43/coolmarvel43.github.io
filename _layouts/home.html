Welcome

This is where I will share everything related to Chinese/Japanese typography, and also document my progress to improve Source Han Sans/Noto Sans CJK and Source Han Serif/Noto Serif CJK.

My Repositories

  • SHS UFO Edits — A collection of modified Source Han Sans/Serif glyphs in UFO and glyphspackage formats. It is mainly to store alternate forms of Traditional Chinese glyphs that are not going to be in the mainline fonts due to glyph policy, and also some design fixes for the font.
